# Code of conduct

Sprout is built in the open by people with different backgrounds, levels of
experience and first languages. Many are contributing to open source for the
first time. We want every one of them to feel welcome to ask, try and get
things wrong.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and their questions.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Critique code and ideas, never people.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it so it can be used.
- Credit others' work.
- Keep discussions on topic and in public, so others can learn from them.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind, including about
  experience level.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly ignoring a request to stop.

## Where this applies

In the Sprout-DevLabs repositories (issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, report it
to the maintainers through GitHub: use **Report content** on the comment or
issue, or report a private concern through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/Sprout-DevLabs/sprout/security) and say
it's a conduct report. Reports are handled privately and as quickly as we can.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who
don't follow this code, and will say why when it's safe to.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
